Our Team

The VARIA Lab is directed by Dr. Lauren Liebe and staffed by students from across Penn State Behrend.

Advisory Board

Our advisory board is comprised of faculty from all four of Behrend's schools, as well as staff members from multiple departments.

Funding

The VARIA Lab is funded by the Penn State Behrend School of Humanities and Social Sciences.

During the 2024-2025 and 2025-2026 academic years, it was also supported by the School of Engineering, the School of Science, the School of Business, and the Chancellor's Office.
